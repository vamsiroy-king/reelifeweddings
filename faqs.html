COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about how we work.

Do you travel for weddings?

Absolutely. We are based in Goa & Bangalore but travel worldwide. From Udaipur to Lake Como, we are ready to fly anywhere your love story takes us.

What is your delivery timeline?

We pride ourselves on speed. You get a set of 10 Social Reels within 24-48 hours. The full cinematic highlights and documentary film are delivered within 4-6 weeks.

Do you provide Raw Footage?

Yes, we believe your memories belong to you. We provide all raw footage on a high-speed SSD upon request at no extra charge (if hard drive is provided by you).

How do we book you?

It is simple. Fill out our Booking Form to check availability. We require a 50% retainer to lock your dates.
