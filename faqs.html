Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our iPhone Content Creation services.

What services do you offer?

We specialize in real-time wedding content creation using top-tier iPhones. This includes shooting aesthetic vertical videos, crafting cinematic reels, and delivering raw footage instantly via AirDrop. We are essentially your personal paparazzi for the day.

How is this different from traditional videography?

Traditional videographers shoot horizontally with large cameras, and their edits take months to deliver. We shoot vertically specifically for social media, blend in like guests, and deliver your edited Reels within 24-48 hours (or even on the same day!).

Do you manage our personal social media account?

Yes, if you choose our Legacy package, we offer complete Social Media Takeovers. We will log into your account, post live stories, upload reels, and engage with your audience so you can just enjoy the wedding without touching your phone.

How much do your services cost?

We offer three transparent tiers: Moments, Signature, and Legacy. Our packages start at ₹9,999 per event. Please fill out our booking form, and we'll send you our detailed brochure.

Will you get in the way of the main photographers?

Absolutely not. We consider ourselves an "additive" service. Because we shoot on iPhones, we are incredibly nimble and unobtrusive. We work *alongside* your main team, never in front of them.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend booking 3 to 6 months in advance, especially during the peak Indian wedding seasons. However, feel free to reach out for last-minute availability!
