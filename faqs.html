Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our iPhone Content Creation services.

What services do you offer?

We specialize in real-time wedding content creation using top-tier iPhones. This includes shooting aesthetic vertical videos, crafting cinematic reels, and delivering raw footage instantly via AirDrop.

How is this different from traditional videography?

Traditional videographers shoot horizontally with large cameras, and their edits take months to deliver. We shoot vertically specifically for social media, blend in like guests, and deliver your edited Reels within 24-48 hours!

Do you manage our personal social media account?

Yes! We offer complete Social Media Takeovers. We will log into your account, post live stories, upload reels, and engage with your audience so you can just enjoy the wedding.

Will you get in the way of the main photographers?

Absolutely not. We consider ourselves an "additive" service. Because we shoot on iPhones, we are incredibly nimble and unobtrusive. We work *alongside* your main team.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend booking 3 to 6 months in advance, especially during the peak Indian wedding seasons.
